Support

Help for Owl on iPhone and iPad: installing from the App Store, breaking goals into to-dos, the evening daily review, pet points, and co-raising.

What’s in Owl

Owl works as one daily loop. A three-question getting-to-know-you interview builds your personal profile. Then you break a goal down into steps with an AI preview, schedule those steps as to-dos, and close the day with an evening daily review and feedback. An owl, cat, or toad companion keeps you company. Finished to-dos earn points that raise the pet’s level.

Installing Owl

Open Owl on the App Store on your iPhone or iPad and tap Get. No TestFlight invitation is needed. Availability depends on your App Store region; newly released versions may take time to appear.

On first launch, choose your language, sign in, and choose an owl, a cat, or a toad as your companion. The three-question getting-to-know-you interview helps build your profile; you can skip questions. Connecting calendars and reminders is optional.

Breaking down goals & scheduling todos

Describe a goal and Owl drafts small steps for it. Review the AI preview before keeping it — nothing is saved until you confirm. Then schedule each step as a todo with a date and time, so the next action is always on your calendar.

Daily Review & feedback summary

Tap Review ready / Review on the Assistant Today card—or open the app from your daily review notification—to review tasks from the current window. You can mark all items completed, uncheck unfinished items before confirming, or tap the undo button right after confirming. The reminder is set to 21:00 by default; change the time or toggle Daily Review on or off in Settings · Daily Review.

Personal profile & Reference Materials

Open Reference Materials from the assistant to inspect which local tasks, projects, or uploaded documents are enabled as context. When remote AI is enabled, only your conversation and the approved reference text are sent through Owl’s official AI gateway; photos and account keys are excluded. You can also review, edit, or forget your three-section Personal Profile in Settings → Personal profile, and toggle whether it is included in Reference Materials.

Pet companions

Choose an owl, a cat, or a toad as your companion. Each one has a Christmas outfit, but it is not included until you redeem it for 300 points.

Pet points and level

Earning points requires a signed-in Owl account. Completing a to-do grants 10 points. A daily check-in grants 5 points on the first day of a streak, then 1 more point for each consecutive day, up to 10. To-do and check-in rewards share a daily cap of 100 points. The pet starts at level 1, and every 100 lifetime points raises the level by 1. Spending points reduces your balance, not the level. Points cannot be bought, cashed out, or exchanged for AI tokens.

The shop also sells extra lines for 100 points and extra motions for 200 points, once each per companion. Owl Pro grants 100 points once a month, in addition to the AI allowance and iCloud sync.

Co-raising

Two signed-in people can raise one pet. One person creates an invite code; it expires after 24 hours. The other person accepts it. While you are paired, the level uses the sum of both people’s lifetime points. Either person can unbind at any time, without the other person’s approval, and Owl does not send a notification for that unbind. After unbinding, the pet stays with the person who created the invite, and each person's level uses only their own lifetime points. Your partner can see your display name, the pet’s name, and your lifetime point total.

Custom pet reservation

You can reserve a custom pet with a species (up to 24 characters), a name (up to 8 characters), up to four personality tags (up to 8 characters each), and a note (up to 200 characters). Submitting needs at least 2,000 available points but spends none; 2,000 points are spent once when the schedule is confirmed. One open reservation per account. You can edit it while it is still only submitted, and cancel it before a schedule is confirmed.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your pet from a snapshot kept on the device. A Lock Screen Live Activity shows your pet and level when a to-do starts.

Owl Pro

Owl Pro is an optional membership. It includes an official AI token allowance, iCloud sync, and 100 pet points each month. On the US App Store it is USD 4.99 per month or USD 34.99 per year; the final price is the one shown in the App Store for your region. If you have a redemption code, enter it in Settings · Owl Pro · Redeem Membership for a 15-day Owl Pro trial. Owl Pro is billed through Apple. You can see your membership status in Settings · Owl Pro in the app; manage or cancel a purchase through Apple’s subscription settings.

Removing local content

Delete individual steps, goals, or reference items inside the app. Deleting the app removes its local container, subject to device backups managed by the user or Apple.

Troubleshooting

App does not launch

Confirm the device runs a supported iOS version and has free storage, then restart the app and device. Avoid deleting the app before securing any backup you need.

Daily Review reminder does not fire

Open Settings · Daily Review to verify that Daily review is toggled on and check that notifications are allowed for Owl in iOS Settings.

Updating without losing local data

Install a new build over the same bundle identifier so local records and preferences are preserved. com.sherry.wizardingos and com.sherry.owl are different apps; installing one does not move the other’s local data.

Pet points do not appear

Points, the shop, a custom-pet reservation, and co-raising require a signed-in Owl account. Check that you are signed in and connected to the internet, and install any available Owl update from the App Store.
