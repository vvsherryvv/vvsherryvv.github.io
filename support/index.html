Help for release 0.4.5

Support

This guide covers Owl 0.4.5 on iPhone, focused on the three-step loop—breaking down goals, scheduling to-dos in Plans, and completing Daily Reviews—along with the 10-step interactive tutorial and Reference Materials control.

What’s in Owl 0.4.5

Version 0.4.5 streamlines the interface around Assistant and Plans. Describe a goal in the assistant (or tap a goal card in Plans to ask AI), inspect and edit the proposed small steps, and confirm to save them. In Plans, swipe a step left to pick a date and start time in the calendar sheet. At your configured Daily Review time, confirm completed tasks or filter unfinished ones. Open Reference Materials to inspect, toggle, or remove the local records the assistant is allowed to read. In Settings → Personal profile (or during welcome setup), you can answer three optional questions to review and save “What I’ve learned about you,” and choose whether to allow that profile in AI reference selection.

Getting started & 10-step tutorial

On first launch, the interactive tutorial bar appears docked above the page so it never covers your conversation. It walks you through entering a real goal—for example: read a book this week, 20 minutes a day—sending it to the assistant, reviewing the draft steps, saving them to Plans, swiping left to schedule a start time, and seeing how the completion circle works.

You can exit or resume the tutorial at any time from the top coach bar or from Settings.

Breaking down goals & scheduling steps

Open the Assistant tab to type or speak a goal, or open the Plans tab to add steps and goals directly. In Plans, tasks are organized into Get Started Easily suggestions, To Start, collapsible Goals & Steps cards, and Completed items. Swipe any step left to reveal the calendar icon (to set its date and start time) and the priority button.

Daily Review & feedback summary

Tap Review ready / Review on the Assistant Today card—or open the app from your daily review notification—to review tasks from the current window. You can mark all items completed, uncheck unfinished items before confirming, or tap the undo button right after confirming. Change the reminder time or toggle Daily Review on or off in Settings · Daily Review.

Reference Materials & upcoming context

Open Reference Materials from the assistant to inspect which local tasks, projects, or uploaded documents are enabled as context. When remote AI is enabled, only your conversation and the approved reference text are sent to the configured AI service; photos and account keys are excluded. You can also review, edit, or forget your three-section Personal Profile in Settings → Personal profile, and toggle whether it is included in Reference Materials.

Exporting and importing JSON backups

Settings can export supported structured records and metadata as JSON. Import uses append-and-deduplicate behavior for supported backup versions, so an item may be skipped when it already exists or cannot be safely read.

Important: user-selected image bytes are not included in JSON backups. Keep original files separately. Exported files leave the app’s control and should be stored or shared carefully.

Removing local content

Delete individual steps, goals, or reference items inside the app. Deleting the app removes its local container, subject to device backups managed by the user or Apple. Export a backup first when records may be needed later.

Accessibility

Owl 0.4.5 supports Dynamic Type (including accessibility text sizes), VoiceOver labels, and Reduce Motion. The top tutorial coach bar and assistant tables scroll cleanly when larger text sizes are enabled.

Offline & network behavior

Viewing and editing local Plans, scheduling dates and times, and completing Daily Reviews work offline on your device. Remote AI goal breakdown, assistant replies, and account verification require a network connection.

Troubleshooting

App does not launch

Confirm the device runs a supported iOS version and has free storage, then restart the app and device. Avoid deleting the app before securing any backup you need.

Daily Review reminder does not fire

Open Settings · Daily Review to verify that Daily review is toggled on and check that notifications are allowed for Owl in iOS Settings.

Import skips items

Confirm the file came from a supported Owl backup version. Existing or duplicate records may be skipped by design.

Upgrade to 0.4.5

Install 0.4.5 over your existing build using the same formal bundle identifier (com.sherry.wizardingos) so local SwiftData records and preferences are preserved.

Privacy
