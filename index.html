How it works

One gentle loop, every day.

Four steps that build on each other — from knowing what matters to you, to looking back on your day.

  1. 1

    Get to know you

    A three-question interview builds your personal profile, so suggestions fit your life. Skip any question you like.

  2. 2

    Break down a goal

    Tell Owl a goal and it drafts small, doable steps. Preview the AI plan first — nothing is saved until you keep it.

  3. 3

    Schedule as todos

    Place each step on your schedule as a todo, so the next action always has a time.

  4. 4

    Evening review

    Wrap up the day with a short daily review: see what got done and get gentle feedback for tomorrow.

Privacy & permissions

Your data stays yours.

Owl only asks for what a feature needs, and you stay in control the whole way.

Read the privacy policy →
  • Every question is skippableThe interview and permission prompts are optional — skip anything and keep using Owl.
  • Authorization you can take backPermissions are requested only when you use a feature, and can be revoked anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Kept on your deviceYour plans and reviews are stored locally. AI requests include only your conversation and the materials you’ve allowed.
