Four inputs

Four sides of your life, together.

Plans and Projects capture how you act. Library and Memory Basin capture what draws you in and what you have lived through.

01 / Work profile

Plans

Make time for what matters.

Schedule daily actions and time blocks, mark completion and look back at your rhythm. Your plans and progress give work mode concrete context.

02 / Work profile

Projects

Turn a goal into a next step.

Organize goals, deadlines and subtasks in Project Scrolls, then add subtasks to your plans. Help your assistant see what you are pursuing and how far you have come.

03 / Personal profile

Library

What you keep reveals what you love.

Keep books, films, papers, webpages and notes with statuses, ratings, excerpts and reflections. Search and confirm a book import, or add an entry yourself.

04 / Personal profile

Memory Basin

Give your experiences a home.

Save memories and journeys with text, photos and travel notes. Recall meaningful moments and give conversations the context of your lived experience.

Two modes · The same you

Context for your work. Room for your world.

The life you record can support different kinds of help at different moments.

Plans + Projects → Work profile → Work mode

Know the goal.
See the progress.

Scheduled time, project deadlines and subtask progress build a grounded work profile. With your selected records, the assistant can clarify goals, break them into steps, help plan and review your work.

  • Break down goals into actionable next steps
  • Plan around existing commitments and available time
  • Review progress within your chosen time range

Library + Memory Basin → Personal profile → Chat mode

Know what you love.
Listen to how you feel.

Books and films you care about, reflections, memories and journeys sketch a personal profile: your interests, preferences and experiences. Conversations can begin with what matters to you.

  • Talk things through and reflect on feelings
  • Explore books and films using clues from your interests
  • Revisit memories and continue meaningful conversations

One reward · Herb Garden

Every record.
A little more growth.

Plans, Projects, Library and Memory Basin each have a plot. Adding a record grows a plant in its plot, making everyday effort visible and giving you something small to look forward to.

  • Four plots reflect the four inputs
  • Harvest a chosen quantity or collect them all
  • Use collected herbs to unlock and choose plants

Record → Grow → Harvest → Keep going

Understanding, with your permission

You choose the context.

Open Reference Records to choose entries by category and inspect their text. Work and Chat modes keep separate permissions and selections. Work mode also lets you set a reference date and time window.

Core records are stored on your device. When using remote AI, your conversation and approved reference text are sent to the AI service you configure. References exclude photos and account keys. You can disable future references; requests already sent cannot be recalled.
