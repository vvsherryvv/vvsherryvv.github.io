OWL · iOS 0.4.5

Break down a goal, schedule the first step, and review each day.

Owl 0.4.5 focuses on a clear three-step loop: turn a goal into small steps with your assistant, place the next step onto your calendar in Plans, and wrap up the day with a lightweight review.

0.4.5 (TestFlight build 68): Streamlined around Assistant & Plans, a 10-step interactive tutorial, Daily Review reminders, and explicit Reference Materials control.

Three-step loop

From a vague intention to a scheduled step you can actually finish.

Every action in 0.4.5 is built around three connected moments—without automatic writes behind your back.

  1. 01

    Break down the goal

    Describe what you want to accomplish in the assistant, or select an existing goal card and choose Ask AI. Review and edit the proposed steps before saving.

  2. 02

    Schedule your to-dos

    Saved goals and steps live in Plans. Swipe any step left to open the calendar picker, set a date and start time, or mark priority items.

  3. 03

    Review and summarize

    Open Review from the Today card or your daily reminder, check off finished items or filter unfinished ones, undo if needed, and ask the assistant for your next step.

01 · Core workflow

Three focused surfaces instead of scattered menus.

Version 0.4.5 removes legacy collection modules and centers the entire app on the Assistant workspace, the Plans storage view, and the Daily Review sheet. Screenshots below are from the Chinese 0.4.5 build.

01 · BREAK DOWN GOALS

Assistant & Today Card

Speak or type a goal, then inspect the draft

The assistant home surfaces today’s scheduled tasks alongside quick actions to start a daily review or find your next step. Nothing is written to Plans until you confirm the preview.

02 · SCHEDULE TO-DOS

Plans & Goals Storage

Keep starter steps, active goals, and scheduled tasks in one place

Plans organizes quick-start suggestions, unscheduled steps, collapsible goal cards, and completed items. Swipe left on any step to set its date and start time.

03 · FEEDBACK & REVIEW

Daily Review Sheet

Confirm what got done and carry unfinished work forward

When your daily review window arrives, check all completed tasks at once or uncheck the ones still in progress, with one-tap undo and configurable reminder times in Settings.

02 · Goal breakdown & scheduling

Preview the plan first, then put the first step on your calendar.

Whether you start from the conversation bar or tap a goal card in Plans, AI breakdown and calendar scheduling stay under your explicit confirmation.

STEP 1 · GOAL BREAKDOWN

Turn a goal into concrete, editable steps.

Ask the assistant to break down a new goal or tap an existing goal card to request AI suggestions. The assistant proposes a structured list of small steps right inside the conversation so you can read, edit, or refine before saving.

  • Confirm whether to break down a goal with AI from the goal card prompt
  • Inspect every proposed step in the draft receipt before saving to Plans
  • Keep multiple goals collapsed or expanded independently with step counts
View goal breakdown confirmation and goals list screenshots

STEP 2 · SCHEDULE & REVIEW

Swipe left to schedule a time, then review at night.

Once a plan is saved, open Plans and swipe any step left to reveal its calendar button and priority toggle. Pick a date and start time, confirm to save, and let Daily Review remind you at your chosen hour (default 21:00).

  • Left-swipe a step to open the date and time picker or mark it important
  • Nothing changes on your schedule until you tap Confirm in the calendar sheet
  • Configure or toggle your Daily Review reminder time anytime in Settings · Daily Review

03 · Interactive tutorial

Walk through your first real goal in 10 guided steps.

First launch includes an interactive coach bar docked above the page so it never blocks your conversation. You can pause, browse around, and return to the current step at any time.

GUIDED ONBOARDING · REAL APP EXAMPLE

“Tap the input and describe a goal. For example: read a book this week, 20 minutes a day.”

Instead of static slides, the 0.4.5 tutorial guides you through creating, saving, and scheduling a real goal of your own—using the built-in reading example (例如：这周开始阅读一本书，每天 20 分钟) as a prompt:

  • Steps 1–4: Enter your goal in the composer, send it to the assistant, review the generated small steps at your own pace, and tap Save
  • Steps 5–8: Open Plans, swipe the highlighted task left to reveal the calendar icon, pick a date and start time (for example 9:00 – 9:20), and tap Confirm
  • Steps 9–10: Learn where to tap the completion circle once you actually finish the step—without forcing you to mark it done prematurely

04 · Reference materials & upcoming roadmap

What we know about you (coming soon), grounded in explicit Reference Materials today.

A dedicated “What we know about you” personal context summary is planned for an upcoming release and is not yet live in 0.4.5. Today in 0.4.5, assistant context is governed through Reference Materials—so you stay in control of what the assistant can read.

Current 0.4.5 behavior vs. upcoming roadmap:

  • Reference Materials (live in 0.4.5): Local-first records stay on your device. You can inspect which tasks, projects, or uploaded reference notes are included, toggle Reference Materials on or off, and remove individual items at any time.
  • No silent uploads: When remote AI is enabled, only the current conversation and the approved reference text are sent to the configured AI service. Reference Materials never include your photo bytes or account keys.
  • “What we know about you” (coming soon): Planned for a future update to help you view and edit summarized personal context before goal breakdown; not claimed as shipped in 0.4.5.
