Break down a goal, schedule the first step, and review each day.

Three-step loop

From a vague intention to a scheduled step you can actually finish.

Every action in 0.4.5 is built around three connected moments—without automatic writes behind your back.

  1. Step 01 · Break Down01

    Break down the goal

    Describe what you want to accomplish in the assistant, or select an existing goal card and choose Ask AI. Review and edit the proposed steps before saving.

  2. Step 02 · Schedule02

    Schedule your to-dos

    Saved goals and steps live in Plans. Swipe any step left to open the calendar picker, set a date and start time, or mark priority items.

  3. Step 03 · Review03

    Review and summarize

    Open Review from the Today card or your daily reminder, check off finished items or filter unfinished ones, undo if needed, and ask the assistant for your next step.

Tip: Swipe horizontally across the window to view all three steps of the action loop.

01 · Core workflow

Three focused surfaces instead of scattered menus.

Version 0.4.5 removes legacy collection modules and centers the entire app on the Assistant workspace, the Plans storage view, and Daily Review. Swipe horizontally to inspect each surface.

01 · BREAK DOWN GOALS

Assistant & Today Card

Speak or type a goal, then inspect the draft

The assistant home surfaces today’s scheduled tasks alongside quick actions to start a daily review or find your next step. Nothing is written to Plans until you confirm the preview.

02 · SCHEDULE TO-DOS

Plans & Goals Storage

Keep starter steps, active goals, and scheduled tasks in one place

Plans organizes quick-start suggestions, unscheduled steps, collapsible goal cards, and completed items. Swipe left on any step to set its date and start time.

03 · FEEDBACK & REVIEW

Today Card & Review Entry

Confirm what got done and carry unfinished work forward

Start your review directly from the Owl assistant Today card or when your daily reminder arrives, checking off finished tasks and carrying unfinished ones forward.

04 · REMINDER SETTINGS

Configurable Daily Review Hour

Toggle reminders and pick your evening check-in time

Enable or pause Daily Review reminders anytime in Settings · Daily Review and customize the exact reminder hour that fits your routine.

Tip: Swipe horizontally across the window to inspect Assistant home, Plans storage, Review entry, and Daily Review settings.

02 · Goal breakdown & scheduling

Preview the plan first, then put the first step on your calendar.

Whether you start from the conversation bar or tap a goal card in Plans, AI breakdown and calendar scheduling stay under your explicit confirmation. Swipe horizontally through all five details.

DETAIL 01 · CONFIRMATION

Ask AI to Break Down a Goal

Explicit confirmation before calling AI

Tapping an existing goal card in Plans asks whether you want AI to break it down first—never overwriting your plan without permission.

DETAIL 02 · DRAFT PREVIEW

Editable Steps in the Assistant

Read and refine every proposed step before saving

The assistant proposes a structured list of small steps right inside the conversation so you can inspect titles and durations before saving to Plans.

DETAIL 03 · GOALS LIST

Collapsible Goal Cards

Expand, collapse, and reorder goals independently

Keep multiple active goals organized in Plans with clear step counts, independent collapse states, and drag-and-drop ordering.

DETAIL 04 · LEFT-SWIPE ACTIONS

Swipe Left to Schedule or Prioritize

Direct actions without buried submenus

Swipe any step row left in Plans to reveal its calendar button and priority exclamation toggle in one motion.

DETAIL 05 · CALENDAR PICKER

Pick a Date and Exact Start Time

Place the step into a real slot on your calendar

Choose a date and toggle on a specific start time in the calendar sheet; nothing changes on your schedule until you tap Confirm.

03 · Interactive tutorial

Walk through your first real goal in 10 guided steps.

First launch includes an interactive coach bar docked above the page so it never blocks your conversation. Swipe horizontally across the window to follow the guided steps in order.

WELCOME · GUIDED ONBOARDING

State a Goal, Save the Plan, Schedule Step 1

Hands-on walkthrough inside the real interface

The welcome card introduces the 10-step guided loop; you can pause to explore freely and resume the current step at any time.

STEP 1 · ENTER A GOAL

Describe What You Want to Finish

“For example: read a book this week, 20 minutes a day.”

The top coach bar prompts you to tap the input field and enter your own goal or try the built-in reading example.

STEP 2 · SEND TO ASSISTANT

Tap the Arrow to Break It Down

STEP 3 · REVIEW THE DRAFT

STEP 4 · SAVE THE PLAN

Confirm and Save to Plans

04 · Get to know you (Personal Profile) & Reference Materials

Build “What I know about you so far” in 3 questions, with explicit Reference Materials control.

Owl 0.4.5 (Build 68) includes a 3-question onboarding interview and editable Personal Profile organized in your own words across rhythm, goals, and support boundaries—governed by explicit toggles in Reference Materials.

01 · 3-QUESTION INTERVIEW

Get to Know You 1/3: How Have Things Been Lately?

Answer by voice transcription, typing, or skip anytime

Three lightweight questions capture your current rhythm, goals, and preferred support style in your own words without storing audio recordings.

02 · PROFILE CONFIRMATION

What I Know About You So Far

Organized in three clear sections, editable at any time

Your answers are organized verbatim across current rhythm, recent goals, and support boundaries—without personality labels—and can be edited or erased anytime.

03 · REFERENCE TOGGLES

Granular Reference Materials Control

Explicit opt-in for Goals, Library files, and Profile

Inspect and toggle whether the assistant may reference your Goals, Library files, or Personal Profile in Reference Materials.

Tip: Swipe horizontally across the window to view the 3-question interview, Personal Profile confirmation, and Reference Materials toggles.

Personal Profile & Reference Materials privacy boundaries:

  • 3-question interview in your own words: Answer by local speech-to-text or typing, or skip any question; raw audio files are never stored.
  • Editable & erasable at any time: Review “What I know about you so far” across three clear sections without personality judgment, and edit or forget your profile anytime in Settings · Personal Profile.
  • Explicit opt-in for AI reference: Profile reference is off by default and is only included alongside Goals and Library files when you explicitly enable it in Reference Materials.
