How it works

One gentle loop, every day.

Four steps that build on each other — from knowing what matters to you, to looking back on your day.

  1. 1

    Get to know you

    A three-question interview builds your personal profile, so suggestions fit your life. Skip any question you like.

  2. 2

    Break down a goal

    Tell Owl a goal and it drafts small, doable steps. Preview the AI plan first — nothing is saved until you keep it.

  3. 3

    Schedule as to-dos

    Place each step on your schedule as a to-do, so the next action always has a time.

  4. 4

    Evening review

    Wrap up the day with a short daily review: see what got done and get gentle feedback for tomorrow.

Pet

Points, a level, and an optional partner.

Earning points, the shop, a custom-pet reservation, and co-raising require a signed-in Owl account. Goals and to-dos stay on your iPhone.

  • Earn points

    Completing a to-do grants 10 points. A daily check-in grants 5 points on the first day of a streak, then 1 more point for each consecutive day, up to 10. To-do and check-in rewards share a daily cap of 100 points. Owl Pro adds 100 points once a month.

  • Level

    The pet starts at level 1. Every 100 lifetime points raises the level by 1. Points you spend come out of your balance, not the level.

  • Spend points

    Redeem a Christmas outfit for 300 points, extra lines for 100, or an extra motion for 200. Points cannot be bought, cashed out, or exchanged for AI tokens.

  • Co-raise

    Two people can raise one pet with an invite code. The code expires after 24 hours. While you are paired, the level uses the sum of both people’s lifetime points, still one level per 100 points. Either person can unbind at any time, without the other person’s approval. After unbinding, the pet stays with the person who created the invite, and each person's level uses only their own lifetime points.

  • Custom pet

    You can reserve a custom pet with a species, a name, up to four personality tags, and a note. Submitting needs at least 2,000 available points but spends none; 2,000 points are spent once when the schedule is confirmed. One open reservation per account. You can edit it while it is still only submitted, and cancel it before a schedule is confirmed.

  • Widgets

    Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your pet from a snapshot kept on the device. A Lock Screen Live Activity shows your pet and level when a to-do starts.

Privacy & permissions

Your goals stay on your iPhone.

Pet points, the shop, reservations, and co-raising are tied to your account. Co-raising shares your display name, the pet, and your lifetime points with the other person.

  • Every question is skippableThe interview and permission prompts are optional — skip anything and keep using Owl.
  • Authorization you can take backPermissions are requested only when you use a feature, and can be revoked anytime in iOS Settings. Either person can unbind a co-raised pet at any time.
  • Goals stay on this iPhoneGoals, to-dos, and reviews are stored locally. Point balances, shop redemptions, custom-pet reservations, and co-raising are stored with your Owl account.
