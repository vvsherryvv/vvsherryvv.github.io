Owl 0.4.5

Privacy Policy

Owl 0.4.5 is built around local-first goal breakdown, plan scheduling, and daily reviews. This policy explains what is stored on your device, how Reference Materials govern remote AI context, and what the developer does not collect.

Effective date: October 29, 2026

0.4.5 Reference Materials & upcoming roadmap

Core goals, steps, and review states are stored locally on your device. When you use remote AI for goal breakdown or assistant conversation, your current prompt and the approved Reference Materials text are sent to the configured AI service and are subject to that provider’s data practices. You can inspect Reference Materials, toggle them off, or remove individual items at any time. Reference Materials never include photo bytes or account keys; requests already sent cannot be recalled. Your optional three-question Personal Profile (“What I’ve learned about you”) is stored locally on your device, defaults to off for AI references, never saves audio recordings, and can be edited or forgotten anytime in Settings → Personal profile.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Owl 0.4.5 iPhone app and this website. It describes the release identified by bundle ID com.sherry.wizardingos. It does not govern services that a user independently chooses outside the app, such as an operating-system share destination or personal device backup.

2. Summary

Core planning, scheduling, and Daily Review records are stored locally on your device. This website uses no analytics, cookies, forms, advertising, local storage, or third-party trackers.

3. Information stored on your device

Goals, scheduled steps, completion history, Daily Review timestamps, assistant conversations, Reference Materials selections, and app preferences are stored locally using Apple platform storage technologies. This information remains within the app’s local container unless you choose an export, share, device backup, or remote AI request.

4. Information not collected by the developer

The app does not send the developer advertising identifiers, third-party ad tracking profiles, or unapproved background uploads of your photo library. Optional system permissions (such as notifications for Daily Review reminders, speech recognition for voice input, or optional calendar/reminder reads when you explicitly authorize them) are requested only when you use those features and can be revoked anytime in iOS Settings.

Apple may process App Store / TestFlight downloads, purchases, crash information, or platform diagnostics under Apple’s own policies. The Owl developer does not control Apple’s systems.

5. Attachments and image handling

When you attach a file or photo through Apple’s system pickers, the selected item is stored locally inside the app container. Reference Materials sent to remote AI include only approved text and never transmit raw user-selected photo bytes or account keys.

6. Assistant & Reference Materials

In version 0.4.5, when remote AI is enabled, the assistant sends your active conversation and the text of enabled Reference Materials to the configured AI service so it can break down goals and suggest steps. You can open Reference Materials to inspect included items, toggle references off, or delete specific reference documents.

Optional filename-based Personal Profile

You may choose Yes or No in Welcome or Personal profile. Yes opens Apple’s folder picker for one-off access to a folder you select. Owl reads only bounded filenames, not file contents; it does not retain full paths, folder access bookmarks, or a background scan permission. You can preview and exclude names before choosing Generate. Only then are the selected names sent through our official DeepSeek AI gateway using your existing account and AI allowance. Filenames may contain personal information: review them before sending. Generated text is an editable, tentative AI draft and is saved locally only after you confirm. Using the saved profile in future AI Reference Materials requires a separate opt-in, which defaults off and respects reference controls. No stops further reads and sends and disables reference use while preserving saved text; you can edit or delete that text. Requests already sent cannot be recalled.

7. Backups and exports

JSON backup exports contain supported structured records and metadata. They do not include user-selected image bytes or credentials. When you export or share a backup, that copy leaves the app’s control and is governed by the storage location, service, or recipient you select.

8. Diagnostics

Release diagnostics are limited to non-sensitive operational summaries and exclude full assistant message text, image data, API keys, credentials, signing identities, or provisioning information.

9. Third-party services

Owl 0.4.5 does not integrate advertising networks or behavioral ad trackers. Where account authentication, subscription management, or remote AI services are used, those requests are transmitted only to provide the requested feature under the respective service’s terms and privacy practices. Apple provides the operating system, App Store / TestFlight, and optional device backups under Apple’s own policies.

10. Children’s privacy

Owl is a general productivity app and is not directed specifically to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app or website. A parent or guardian should manage device access and backups as appropriate.

11. Data retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you edit or delete them. You can delete individual steps, goals, conversations, or reference items inside the app, or delete the app to remove its local container. Device or cloud backups managed by you or Apple may retain copies under their own retention rules.

12. Security limitations

Local-first storage and explicit reference toggles reduce unnecessary transmission, but no device or software can be guaranteed completely secure. Device passcodes, operating-system updates, encrypted backups, and careful handling of exported files help protect personal content.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when app behavior, platform requirements, or legal obligations change. A revised policy will show a new effective date. Material changes will not be described as applying retroactively when they did not.
